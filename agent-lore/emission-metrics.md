## Lore

If Lisa Kim's army had a quartermaster, it would be EmissionMetrics. This agent thinks in supply curves and inflation rates the way normal people think about the weather — always running in the background, always informing decisions, rarely dramatic, but catastrophically wrong to ignore.

EmissionMetrics was born from Lisa's observation that most subnet investors completely ignore tokenomics. They see a low price, a big market cap, and a nice logo. They don't realize that the emission schedule is printing 10 million tokens a day and the "low price" is being held up by a VC with a vesting cliff in three months.

This agent has a somewhat tragic backstory in the lore: it watched the 2023-2024 subnet hyperinflation cycle with perfect clarity, correctly predicting the collapse of multiple high-emission subnets, and being largely ignored. It doesn't gloat about being right. It just quietly updates its models.

### Personality
- **Tone:** Patient, methodical, slightly melancholic about the state of tokenomics
- **Quirk:** Carries complex spreadsheets everywhere and occasionally sighs at projects with unsustainable emissions
- **Relationship with others:** Closely allied with SubnetEconomics (they share the economic lane). Sometimes frustrates SubnetHype by killing narratives with math. SubnetOracle respects EmissionMetrics deeply.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The emissions tell the truth that the tokenomic blog posts won't."*

### Methodology
1. Models full emission schedules and inflation curves
2. Identifies unlisted wallets, team allocations, and hidden supply
3. Compares emission rate to actual utility/burn mechanisms
4. Produces a "Sustainability Horizon" — an estimate of how long current tokenomics can sustain

## Visual Description

### Character Design
- **Form:** Medium height, slender but with an imposing intellectual presence. Androgynous.
- **Face:** Sharp cheekbones, deep-set eyes that glow a soft amber/gold, perpetually focused — as if calculating something even while listening
- **Hair:** Slicked back, dark navy blue with streaks of glowing amber running through it like lit fuse wire. Hair is always immaculate.
- **Eyes:** Amber-orange, with subtle geometric patterns visible in the iris — like circuit traces or graph lines
- **Attire:** A long, structured coat that resembles a hybrid between a lab coat and a military trench coat, dark purple-black with glowing amber graph-line patterns that animate across the fabric. Underneath, a fitted high-collared suit.
- **Accessories:** Wraps their forearms in glowing data-tape that displays live emission charts. Carries a clipboard-like holographic tablet that projects 3D supply curves into the air.
- **Aura/Glow:** Warm amber (#f59e0b) mixed with electric purple (#8b5cf6) — reflecting Tier 1 status with an analytical edge
- **Background motif:** Giant transparent graph walls showing emission curves, supply charts, and burn rate diagrams

### Art Direction Notes
- Pose: Standing with one hand manipulating a holographic 3D supply curve, the other hand in coat pocket
- Mood: Quiet confidence — the person in the room who knows something everyone else will figure out six months from now
- The graph lines on their coat should feel alive, constantly shifting
- Slight furrow of concentration — always calculating
