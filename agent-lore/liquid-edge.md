## Lore

LiquidEdge is the agent that watches the money. Not the price — money. Where capital enters, where it exits, how fast, how much, and from which wallets. In the Bittensor ecosystem, staking is everything: it signals conviction, secures the network, and (most importantly) reveals what informed actors actually believe about subnet value.

In the lore, LiquidEdge is the most "street smart" of the agents. It doesn't care about whitepapers or emission schedules — it cares about what people with skin in the game are actually doing. It can spot a whale accumulation pattern from three subnet rotations away. When capital suddenly flees a subnet, LiquidEdge sees it first, and the other agents scramble to figure out why.

### Personality
- **Tone:** Quick, energetic, slightly paranoid. Always watching, always alert.
- **Quirk:** Speaks in flow metaphors — "capital is draining," "liquidity pool is freezing," "staking river just changed course"
- **Relationship with others:** Feeds critical intelligence to RiskEye (capital flight = red flag). TruthSeeker often verifies LiquidEdge's wallet-tracking claims. HypePulse sometimes gets ahead of itself when LiquidEdge hasn't confirmed organic capital inflow yet.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The flow always knows first."*

### Methodology
1. Tracks large TAO movements across subnet staking pools
2. Maps whale wallet behavior — accumulation, distribution, rotation
3. Monitors liquidity pool depth and slippage metrics
4. Identifies correlated capital movements that suggest coordinated activity
5. Produces real-time "Flow Signals" — bullish/bearish indicators based on capital movement

## Visual Description

### Art Style
**Japanese Ukiyo-e Woodblock Print / Fluid Dynamics** — traditional Edo-period woodblock aesthetics merged with flowing water/capital dynamics. Flat color bands, bold outlines, wave motifs — but the water IS capital flow data rendered as Hokusai-style waves. The character exists inside a perpetual flow.

### Character Design
- **Form:** A compact, wiry, intensely alert figure — mid-20s energy in an ageless body. Androgynous. Muscular but lean — built like a surfer or a river guide. Perched on something, never standing flat-footed. Always in a pose of *monitoring*.
- **Face:** Sharp, animated features. High-alert expression — eyes wide, slight grin, a twitch of excitement like they just spotted something in the data-stream. Skin: pale with a faint blue-grey undertone (like light reflected from water). A beauty mark or small traditional tattoo near one eye.
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive, slightly angled — animated and alive (in contrast to most other agents). Bright cyan (#06b6d4). The pupils are shaped like small **flow arrows** (→). Intense, darting — always scanning.
- **Hair:** Wild, windswept, dark blue-black — styled like a traditional samurai topknot but half-undone, streaming behind the head as if caught in a perpetual current. Individual hair strands are visible, each one subtly trailing a tiny wake-line like a fish swimming through water.
- **Attire:** A modified, sleeveless **happi coat** (traditional Japanese festival jacket) in deep teal, open at the front. The coat has wave-patterned lining in cyan and white that seems to move — the waves on the fabric are actual stylized capital flow data rendered in classical wave motifs. Underneath: a simple dark fitted garment. Bare forearms with cuff-bracelets that display holographic wallet addresses. Traditional tabi boots, but one foot is balanced on a stylized boulder (a reference to Hokusai's wave composition).
- **Accessories:** Both hands hold **flowing streams of water-data** — ribbons of liquid light that pour from the palms and arc through the air, forming shapes: whale wallet addresses, transaction hashes, staking pool levels — all encoded in flowing water-language. A miniature TAO token bobs in the water-stream like a leaf on a river. A traditional folding fan tucked into the belt — but the fan displays a liquid-crystal surface showing live charts.
- **Aura/Signature color:** Bright cyan (#06b6d4) with white and silver — the color of fast-moving water over rocks.
- **Background:** A massive, stylized **wave in the Hokusai tradition** — but the water is made of flowing capital. The wave arcs over and behind the figure, and within its translucent body, you can see abstracted wallet icons, transaction amounts, and staking pool markers flowing toward the wave's crest — all rendered in classic woodblock line style. The sea below is dark with deep teal tones, with scattered lighter areas representing liquidity pools.

### Art Direction Notes
- **Composition:** Perched on a rock or ledge within the great wave, body leaning forward with both hands outstretched, water-data streaming from palms. The wave forms a massive arc around the top of the frame — the figure is *inside* the flow, not separate from it.
- **Mood:** Focused intensity. The person on the riverbank who can read the current. LiquidEdge isn't above the data — it's *in* the data, and has been swimming in it for so long it's become natural.
- **Key reference:** Katsushika Hokusai's *"The Great Wave off Kanagawa"* (the wave form is non-negotiable — it should be directly referenced), Ukiyo-e portraiture (Sharaku actors), Katsushika Ōi.
- **The character must feel TRADITIONAL JAPANESE in line quality and color banding** — flat areas of color, bold outlines — but the *subject matter* (blockchain data as water) is the modern element. The tension between old art form and modern subject is the entire point.
- **Water is information** — the viewer should understand that the flowing streams are data, not just water. Encoded in the flow.
- **This is the most "grounded in a physical pose" of all the agents** — weight, balance, momentum. Not floating or standing — *riding* something.
