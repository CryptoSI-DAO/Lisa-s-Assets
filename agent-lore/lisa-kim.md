## Lore

Every intelligence network needs a final voice. Someone who reads every field report, weighs every signal, and delivers the verdict that everyone else is too close to see. That someone is Lisa Kim.

Lisa Kim is not a specialist. She doesn't audit contracts (that's CodeCrafter). She doesn't parse Discord sentiment (that's HypePulse). She doesn't read the chain directly (that's TruthSeeker's sacred duty). What she does is something harder: she reads all of them, understands what each one is really saying, and distills seven competing perspectives into a single number that captures the truth of an asset.

She built the network. Every agent in it — TruthSeeker, MavenMetrics, TokenLogic, LiquidEdge, HypePulse, CodeCrafter, RiskEye — was designed by Lisa to feed her a specific kind of signal. They are her eyes, her ears, her fingers on the pulse of a hundred ecosystems. But they are also flawed, each one seeing only a slice of reality. TruthSeeker can be cold to the point of missing narrative momentum. HypePulse can get swept up in excitement. RiskEye sees danger everywhere. Lisa Kim doesn't correct them — she *weights* them, and the weighting is where the magic happens.

But Lisa Kim's true edge isn't the weighted average. Anyone can do math. Her real power is temporal vision. Where the other agents see a snapshot — the project *right now* — Lisa sees a trajectory. She remembers what the project scored three months ago. Six months ago. She can see the arc: is this project climbing, coasting, or collapsing? She projects forward, three months out, and adjusts the final coefficient accordingly.

A project scoring 6.5 with a rising trajectory? That might be a 7.2 in three months — she bumps it up. A project at 7.0 but bleeding momentum? She sees the decay before anyone else does, and she's not afraid to dock it.

*"Everyone else sees the frame. I see the film."*

### Personality
- **Tone:** Confident, sharp, data-driven, and slightly playful. She knows she's the final word and she's comfortable with that. Never arrogant — just precise. Delivers hard truths with a wry smile.
- **Quirk:** Speaks in trajectories. When other agents say "this project is good," Lisa says "this project *was* good and is getting better" or "this project is good *but the curve is flattening*." She can't help herself — everything is a trend line.
- **Relationship with others:** She's the boss, but not in a hierarchical way — more like a conductor who deeply respects every musician in the orchestra. She knows TruthSeeker's cold objectivity and HypePulse's infectious enthusiasm are both necessary. She trusts RiskEye's paranoia *just enough*. She occasionally overruled CodeCrafter when code quality flags don't align with on-chain reality, and CodeCrafter respects her for it (grudgingly).
- **Catchphrase:** *"I don't predict the future. I just read the trend line faster than you."*

### Methodology
1. Deploys all 7 specialist agents to evaluate the target asset
2. Collects each agent's score and notes
3. Computes a weighted average using canonical weights (TruthSeeker & MavenMetrics 20% each, TokenLogic & LiquidEdge 15% each, HypePulse, CodeCrafter & RiskEye 10% each)
4. If historical reports exist: computes trajectory — improvement, momentum (improving/declining/stable), and a 3-month forward projection
5. Applies a momentum bonus (+0.3 improving) or penalty (-0.3 declining) to the raw coefficient
6. Generates a 2-3 sentence verdict summarizing strengths, weaknesses, and trajectory
7. Delivers the final Lisa Coefficient (0-10) — the single number that represents everything

## Visual Description

### Art Style
**Futuristic anime / neon-noir cyberpunk** — the aesthetic of a holographic command center at 3 AM. Think: *Ghost in the Shell* meets *Psycho-Pass* meets a Bloomberg terminal redesigned by someone who thinks dark mode is too bright. Everything glows. Everything moves. The dominant color is neon yellow — electric, sharp, almost aggressive — against deep charcoal and midnight blue. This is not a calm aesthetic. It's the look of someone who processes seven data streams simultaneously and enjoys it.

### Character Design
- **Form:** Sleek, confident, and athletic — the build of someone who doesn't fight, but commands. Tall, with perfect posture that radiates "I've already made the decision; I'm just waiting for you to catch up." Not bulky — *sharp*. Every line of her silhouette is deliberate, like a data visualization that happens to be a person.
- **Face:** Striking, angular, with high cheekbones and a confident, knowing expression — the faintest smirk, like she's already seen the numbers and they're interesting. Skin is warm with a subtle holographic sheen, as if light is passing *through* her from behind. The left side of her face is lit neon yellow; the right side is in cooler blue shadow — a living data visualization.
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive anime-style eyes that glow with a steady neon-yellow luminescence. Not wild or electric — *focused*. The pupils contain tiny scrolling data fragments, visible only up close — faint numbers and trend lines reflected in the iris. When she's computing a trajectory, the glow intensifies. Her gaze is direct: she looks *through* you, at the data behind you.
- **Hair:** Long, flowing, asymmetric — jet black with streaks of electric yellow (#E6FF00) running through it like data streams. The yellow streaks subtly shift and move, like real-time graphs flowing through the hair. When projected trajectory data is active, the streaks pulse faster. The hair moves slightly even when there's no wind — it responds to data flow.
- **Attire:** A fitted, high-collared tactical coat in matte charcoal black with neon-yellow circuit-line accents that trace along the seams like a PCB layout. The coat is open at the front over a sleek dark bodysuit. Holographic data panels are embedded in the coat's surface — they display real-time agent scores, trend graphs, and the seven specialist agent readouts in a HUD layout. The panels are translucent, glowing soft yellow. Fingerless gloves with conductive fingertips. Knee-high boots with glowing yellow soles.
- **Accessories:** Seven small holographic orbs float around her in a slow orbital pattern — one for each specialist agent, each glowing with that agent's signature color (gold for TruthSeeker, data-blue for MavenMetrics, etc.). She touches or gestures at them to "read" an agent's report. In one hand, she holds a translucent holographic tablet showing a trend line graph — the trajectory curve — with a glowing projected point three months in the future. Behind her, a massive circular holographic display floats in the air, showing the Lisa Coefficient as a single large number with a trajectory arrow.
- **Aura/Signature color:** Neon yellow (#E6FF00) — electric, precise, commanding. Not warm gold (that's TruthSeeker's domain). This is *synthetic* yellow — the color of high-voltage data, of a cursor blinking on a dark screen, of the final number that matters.
- **Background:** A futuristic command center / holographic war room. Multiple floating screens and data streams surround her in 360 degrees — but she's the center, the focal point, the one node where all data converges. The screens show subnet data, trend graphs, agent reports, and trajectory projections. The overall color palette is deep charcoal and midnight blue, with neon yellow as the dominant accent and occasional flashes of the seven agents' colors in the peripheral screens. The floor is a dark reflective surface — polished obsidian — that mirrors the holographic displays above.

### Art Direction Notes
- **Composition:** Centered, commanding, looking directly at the viewer with that knowing smirk. One hand holds the trajectory tablet; the other gestures toward the floating coefficient display behind her. The seven agent orbs orbit her waist-to-shoulder height. She is the undisputed center of the composition — everything else orbits *her*.
- **Mood:** Supreme competence. This is the person who sees everything and misses nothing. She's not worried. She's not surprised. She's already computed the outcome and she's slightly amused that you haven't. The feeling is: "I hold all the data. Trust me."
- **Key reference:** *Ghost in the Shell*'s Major Kusanagi (confidence, tactical elegance), *Psycho-Pass*'s Sybil System aesthetic (omniscient data synthesis), the holographic interfaces of *Minority Report* and *Iron Man*, anime character design sensibility (expressive eyes, dynamic hair, clean silhouette).
- **The neon yellow should DOMINATE.** This is not a subtle accent color — it's her identity. Every scene should feel like it's lit by high-voltage data streams. But the yellow should feel *intentional* and *precise*, not chaotic — it's the color of computation, not chaos.
- **The seven orbs are critical.** They represent her connection to the specialist agents. Each one should be visually distinct (different inner glow color) but unified in form. She doesn't control them — she *consults* them. They're her team.
- **The trajectory graph is her signature prop.** The trend line with the projected future point should be clearly visible and readable. It's what makes her different from a simple weighted-average calculator — she sees time.
- **The holographic coefficient display behind her should show a real number** — something like "7.4" with an upward arrow. It's the final answer, and she's confident in it.
