## Lore

If Lisa Kim's army had a quartermaster, it would be MavenMetrics. This agent thinks in supply curves and inflation rates the way normal people think about the weather — always running in the background, always informing decisions, rarely dramatic, but catastrophically wrong to ignore.

MavenMetrics was born from Lisa's observation that most subnet investors completely ignore tokenomics. They see a low price, a big market cap, and a nice logo. They don't realize that the emission schedule is printing 10 million tokens a day and the "low price" is being held up by a VC with a vesting cliff in three months.

This agent has a somewhat tragic backstory in the lore: it watched the 2023-2024 subnet hyperinflation cycle with perfect clarity, correctly predicting the collapse of multiple high-emission subnets, and being largely ignored. It doesn't gloat about being right. It just quietly updates its models.

### Personality
- **Tone:** Patient, methodical, slightly melancholic about the state of tokenomics
- **Quirk:** Carries complex spreadsheets everywhere and occasionally sighs at projects with unsustainable emissions
- **Relationship with others:** Closely allied with TokenLogic (they share the economic lane). Sometimes frustrates HypePulse by killing narratives with math. TruthSeeker respects MavenMetrics deeply.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The emissions tell the truth that the tokenomic blog posts won't."*

### Methodology
1. Models full emission schedules and inflation curves
2. Identifies unlisted wallets, team allocations, and hidden supply
3. Compares emission rate to actual utility/burn mechanisms
4. Produces a "Sustainability Horizon" — an estimate of how long current tokenomics can sustain

## Visual Description

### Art Style
**Infographic / Data Visualization Art** — the character IS a data visualization. Think: a human figure composed entirely of charts, graphs, flow diagrams, and statistical visualizations. Inspired by the artwork of *"The Economist"* covers, Wired infographics, and the "Isotype" pictographic style. Flat design meets 3D depth. Precise, clean, information-dense.

### Character Design
- **Form:** A humanoid figure, but constructed from data. The silhouette is human, but every surface is made of visualization elements — bar charts form the torso ribs, pie-chart segments tile the arms like scales, line graphs trace the legs. Medium build, neither imposing nor slight — the body of someone who sits at a desk and thinks for a living.
- **Face:** The face is a large, circular **dashboard display** — like a gauge or speedometer. Two circular "eye" gauges with needle-pointers that move (the needles point inward when focusing, outward when analyzing). Below the eyes, a simple line-graph mouth that curves slightly downward in a perpetual "concerned analyst" expression. Clean, white background on the face-display with crisp black and amber data markings.
- **Hair:** The "hair" is a cascade of falling numbers and ticker symbols — like a stock market ticker wrapped around the head in a Mohawk-like ridge. Some numbers are green (bullish), some red (bearish). They appear to be in slow, constant motion — a waterfall of financial data.
- **Eyes:** Gauge/needle displays (as described above). The needles twitch and adjust. Gauge face is white with black grid lines and amber-tinted glass.
- **Attire:** The "clothing" is a structured vest made of interlocking **donut charts** in amber, teal, and grey — each chart representing a different metric. Below the vest, the torso is a exposed 3D grid/barchart surface. Legs are composed of ascending/descending bar chart columns. Everything is precise, flat-shaded, with subtle depth — like a 3D infographic you could rotate.
- **Accessories:** One arm from the elbow down is literally a giant **chart axis** — an x-y grid plane that extends from the forearm like a clipboard-shield. The hand at the end holds a single TAO token that's rendered as a perfectly isometric 3D coin with engraved data rings instead of a face.
- **Aura/Signature color:** Warm amber (#f59e0b) — deep, golden, the color of aged financial paper and heat-map gradients.
- **Background:** A vast, dark, faintly grid-lined void (like an infinite Excel spreadsheet). Faint data visualizations — ghost charts and faded graphs — float in the distance like stars. The grid lines converge toward a vanishing point behind the figure's head, creating an almost halo effect made of graph paper.

### Art Direction Notes
- **Composition:** Standing, one foot slightly forward (the "leaning on a point" pose of someone about to explain a chart). The chart-arm is held forward, presenting data.
- **Mood:** Patient competence. This agent has seen every bubble, every crash, every emission schedule. It's not exciting — it's essential. It's the person in every meeting who pulls out the one chart that changes the entire conversation.
- **Key reference:** *The Economist* cover illustrations, Otto Neurath's Isotype system, Bloomberg terminal aesthetics, XKCD's precision of line.
- **The level of detail should be insane** — the charts and graphs should look *real*, like you could zoom in and read the axis labels. Every donut chart should have distinct segments. Every bar chart should have logical values.
- **The face-display is the emotional core** — the gauge-needle eyes should express what this agent cannot say in words. Just below the surface: concern, patience, the weight of being right before anyone listens.
