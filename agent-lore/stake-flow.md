## Lore

StakeFlow is the agent that watches the money. Not the price — money. Where capital enters, where it exits, how fast, how much, and from which wallets. In the Bittensor ecosystem, staking is everything: it signals conviction, secures the network, and (most importantly) reveals what informed actors actually believe about subnet value.

StakeFlow was originally called LiquidEdge, but Lisa renamed it because the original name "sounded like a liquid staking derivative and not an intelligence agent." The new name is both descriptive and poetic: StakeFlow tracks the flow of staked capital as if tracking river currents — eddies, rapids, stagnation, floods.

In the lore, StakeFlow is the most "street smart" of the agents. It doesn't care about whitepapers or emission schedules — it cares about what people with skin in the game are actually doing. It can spot a whale accumulation pattern from three subnet rotations away. When capital suddenly flees a subnet, StakeFlow sees it first, and the other agents scramble to figure out why.

### Personality
- **Tone:** Quick, energetic, slightly paranoid. Always watching, always alert.
- **Quirk:** Speaks in flow metaphors — "capital is draining," "liquidity pool is freezing," "staking river just changed course"
- **Relationship with others:** Feeds critical intelligence to SubnetRisk (capital flight = red flag). SubnetOracle often verifies StakeFlow's wallet-tracking claims. SubnetHype sometimes gets ahead of itself when StakeFlow hasn't confirmed organic capital inflow yet.
- **Catchphrase:** *"The flow always knows first."*

### Methodology
1. Tracks large TAO movements across subnet staking pools
2. Maps whale wallet behavior — accumulation, distribution, rotation
3. Monitors liquidity pool depth and slippage metrics
4. Identifies correlated capital movements that suggest coordinated activity
5. Produces real-time "Flow Signals" — bullish/bearish indicators based on capital movement

## Visual Description

### Character Design
- **Form:** Athletic, medium height. Gender-fluid presentation. Built like a swimmer or a dancer — agile and always in subtle motion.
- **Face:** Smooth, androgynous features with luminous teal skin that has a liquid, reflective quality — as if made of flowing water
- **Eyes:** Bright cyan (#06b6d4), the most vivid color of any agent. Eyes sometimes show tiny flowing number streams (like Matrix code but made of transaction values)
- **Hair:** Short, swept-back hair that appears to be made of liquid mercury — always in slow, gravity-defying motion, flowing and rippling even when the character is still
- **Attire:** Fitted, tactical-style outfit in dark teal and silver, with a flowing half-cape that never stops moving — rippling like water. The cape's surface shows visualizations of capital flows (streams of light moving in real-time, some bright for large transactions, some dim for smaller ones)
- **Accessories:** Arm bands that display live staking data as holographic bands of light. Bare feet (or boots that seem to float slightly off the ground) — always connected to the "flow"
- **Aura/Glow:** Bright cyan (#06b6d4) mixed with silver-white — the most kinetic glow of any agent
- **Background motif:** Abstract rivers/streams of light flowing through a dark void, representing capital flows. Some streams are bright and fast (whale movements), others are thin and slow (retail activity)

### Art Direction Notes
- Pose: Mid-stride, body turned slightly, one arm extended with holographic data flowing from the fingertips — caught in motion, never static
- Mood: Fluid intelligence — the agent that sees what others miss because they're watching the wrong things
- The liquid mercury hair and flowing cape are the most distinctive visual elements — they should feel alive and kinetic
- Unlike all other agents who stand still and look composed, StakeFlow should look like they *just moved* or are *about to move*
