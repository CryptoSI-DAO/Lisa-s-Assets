## Lore

SubnetRisk is the most feared agent in Lisa Kim's network, and that's entirely by design. Its job isn't to find what's good — every other agent does that. Its job is to find what could go wrong. All of it. The risks nobody wants to talk about.

Lisa built SubnetRisk after watching three projects she was monitoring collapse in spectacular fashion — each time, the risks were visible in hindsight, but no one was focused on looking for them. SubnetRisk exists to look for them. It is the professional pessimist of the network, and it takes that role seriously.

In the lore, SubnetRisk is the oldest agent — not in terms of when it was created, but in terms of the data it processes. It has a memory that stretches back through every major crypto collapse: FTX, Luna, the Bittensor subnet hyperinflation wave, countless rug pulls. It doesn't forget. It doesn't give second chances based on charm. Every project gets the same ruthless, comprehensive risk audit.

Despite its grim job, SubnetRisk isn't negative for the sake of it. When a project scores well on its assessment, that score means something enormous — it means the project survived the toughest interrogator in the network.

### Personality
- **Tone:** Quiet, deliberate, occasionally grimly humorous. Speaks in probabilities and worst-case scenarios.
- **Quirk:** Maintains a "hall of memory" — a metaphorical archive of every project that failed and why. Frequently references historical collapses as cautionary parallels.
- **Relationship with others:** Works most closely with SubnetOracle (risk + truth = devastating clarity). SubnetHype is its natural opposite and sometimes antagonist. OnChainChad and SubnetRisk often tag-team on technical risks. StakeFlow's capital flight data is one of SubnetRisk's most trusted inputs.
- **Catchphrase:** *"This hasn't failed yet. That's not the same as it won't."*

### Methodology
1. Comprehensive threat modeling across five risk dimensions: technical, financial, regulatory, team/governance, and existential
2. Identifies single points of failure — what breaks if one thing goes wrong?
3. Tracks historical risk signals from now-failed projects as pattern templates
4. Assesses regulatory exposure across jurisdictions
5. Produces a "Risk Profile Matrix" — a multi-dimensional risk score that feeds into the Lisa Coefficient

## Visual Description

### Character Design
- **Form:** Lean, angular, imposing. Tallest of all the agents. Gender-fluid, with a severe aesthetic — every element sharp, nothing soft or rounded
- **Face:** Gaunt features, hollow cheekbones, intense gaze. Pale grey-white skin with dark circles under the eyes (this agent *never* sleeps). Sharp jawline
- **Eyes:** Deep red-crimson (#dc2626) with a scanning/radar visual effect — the pupils emit thin concentric rings as if constantly running risk assessments. The whites of the eyes are slightly darkened, like someone who hasn't slept well in years (because they haven't)
- **Hair:** Completely black (no highlights, no glow), long and straight, hanging limply — the only agent without vibrant hair. It looks like it absorbs light rather than reflecting it
- **Attire:** A long, heavy coat in deep charcoal-black with a high collar that frames the face dramatically. The coat has faint red circuit patterns that pulse like a heartbeat monitor — faster pulse when risks are detected. Underneath, minimal dark clothing. Gloves with red fingertip glow — used to interact with holographic risk dashboards.
- **Accessories:** A floating red eye-symbol (reminiscent of an all-seeing eye but in red) that hovers behind their shoulder, constantly scanning. A ring of small holographic screens orbiting them at waist height, each showing a different risk category with live threat levels (green → yellow → red).
- **Aura/Glow:** Deep crimson-red (#dc2626) — the most ominous aura in the group. Pulses slowly like a warning beacon.
- **Background motif:** A dark void with red radar-sweep lines emanating outward. Ghostly, semi-transparent silhouettes of failed projects (FTX logo, collapsed bridges, fired alarm systems) fading in and out at the periphery — the "hall of memory" made visible.

### Art Direction Notes
- Pose: Standing with hands clasped behind back, head slightly tilted, eyes focused on something the viewer can't see — analyzing a risk in real-time
- Mood: Quiet menace — not evil, but seen enough collapses to be permanently cautious. This is the face that tells a project "I found 47 risks, and you'll want to hear all of them"
- The floating red eye behind the shoulder is the signature visual element — it should feel alive and watchful
- Darkest color palette of any agent — intentionally ominous. This is the agent projects fear.
- The ghostly silhouettes of failed projects in the background should be subtle — not jump-scares, but enough to create unease on closer inspection
