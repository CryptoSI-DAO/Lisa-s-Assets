## Ecosystem Overview

Ethereum is the foundational smart contract layer of crypto. With ~$50B+ in DeFi TVL, it remains the undisputed home of on-chain finance — despite rising competition from Solana, Hyperliquid, and others. Ethereum's moat is threefold: deepest liquidity, most battle-tested protocols, and the largest developer ecosystem.

**Key milestone:** ETH priced at ~$1,665 (June 2026), down from the 2024 highs near $4,000 but stabilizing. The narrative has shifted from "ultra-sound money" to "real yield infrastructure" — and Ethereum DeFi delivers exactly that.

### What Makes Ethereum Different

| Feature | Ethereum | Solana | Hyperliquid | L2s (Base, Arb) |
|---------|----------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| TVL | ~$50B+ | ~$8B | ~$1.6B | ~$20B combined |
| Validators | 900K+ | ~2,000 | 27 | Varies |
| Finality | ~15 min | ~0.4 sec | ~1 sec | Varies |
| EVM | Native | No (SVM) | Yes (HyperEVM) | Yes |
| Decentralization | Highest | Medium | Low | Medium-High |

### The Flywheel

ETH staking yield → LSTs enable DeFi composability → More TVL → More fees → ETH burns → Scarcer ETH → Higher staking yield → More staking. Post-merge, Ethereum has been natively yield-bearing — and the rest of DeFi is built on top of that base layer.

## Tokenomics

### ETH Token
- **Supply:** ~120M ETH (no hard cap post-merge)
- **Issuance:** ~0.5% annual (post-merge, down from ~4% pre-merge)
- **Burn mechanism:** EIP-1559 burns base fees → deflationary during high activity
- **Staking yield:** ~3.5% base (consensus layer) + MEV + L2 rewards
- **Key insight:** ETH is the only major crypto asset that is *natively yield-bearing* without any protocol wrapper. Staking ETH directly earns ~3.5% in new ETH.

### The LST Dominance Question
Lido controls ~50% of staked ETH — a centralization risk that the community actively debates. Rocket Pool (decentralized alternative) has grown but remains at ~3% share. DVT (Distributed Validator Technology) via Obol and SSV is the emerging solution.

## Opportunities Summary

### 🟢 High Conviction
1. **Lido (stETH)** — Dominant LST with $14.84B TVL, ~50% market share, and deep DeFi composability. The "default" way to earn ETH staking yield while maintaining liquidity. Centralization concerns are real but the moat is enormous.
2. **Aave** — The lending blue chip. $12.30B TVL, omnichain expansion (Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana), and GHO stablecoin creating internal demand. AAVE token captures revenue via safety module staking.

### 🔷 Research Further
3. **Sky (Maker)** — Rebranded Maker with USDS stablecoin and real-world asset (RWA) yield. $6.03B TVL, but the transition from DAI to USDS and the RWA-heavy model introduces new risks. High yield (DSR ~5-8%) attracts capital.
4. **Pendle** — Yield tokenization is a genuinely new primitive. $1.17B TVL with explosive growth potential as more yield-bearing assets emerge. PENDLE tokenomics are improving with fee sharing.
5. **Ethena (USDe)** — Delta-neutral synthetic dollar with $4.84B TVL. The "cash and carry" strategy generates real yield, but depends on perpetual funding rates staying positive. High risk/reward.

### 🟡 Watch
6. **Rocket Pool** — The decentralized LST alternative. $0.89B TVL is modest vs. Lido, but the node operator model is genuinely more decentralized. RPL token is deeply undervalued (MCap $31M). Needs DVT adoption to scale.
7. **EigenLayer** — Restaking is a powerful new primitive, but $4.64B TVL (-36.7% 30d) shows the hype has cooled. AVS marketplace is still early. EIGEN token utility is unclear.

### 🔴 Avoid (For Most)
- Most new L2 tokens — emissions-driven, no real revenue
- Forks of existing protocols with no differentiation
- Any protocol with >50% of yield from token emissions
- Unaudited or anonymous-team protocols
