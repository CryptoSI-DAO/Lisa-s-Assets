## 🔍 TruthSeeker — 6/10

**What It Does:** EigenLayer introduces "restaking" — ETH stakers can opt-in to secure additional services (AVS — Actively Validated Services) beyond Ethereum consensus. This lets new protocols bootstrap security from Ethereum's validator set instead of building their own. Users restake ETH/stETH → earn additional yield from AVS fees.

**Comparable to:** The "security-as-a-service" layer of Ethereum — like AWS for blockchain security.

**Fundamental Analyst Notes:**
- Restaking is a genuinely new primitive — no one else does this at scale
- The AVS marketplace (bridges, oracles, data availability layers) is the vision
- However, AVS adoption has been slower than expected — most TVL is just "points farming"
- -36.7% 30d TVL decline shows the hype has cooled and capital is leaving
- The EIGEN token was airdropped but utility is unclear — governance + slashing insurance
