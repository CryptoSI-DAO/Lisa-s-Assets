## 🔍 TruthSeeker — 6/10

**What It Does:** Ethena issues USDe, a synthetic dollar backed by a delta-neutral position: long ETH staking yield + short ETH perpetual futures. The "cash and carry" trade generates yield (~10-20% historically, now ~5-8%) that is distributed to sUSDe stakers. USDe is not backed by fiat or crypto collateral — it's backed by the funding rate differential.

**Comparable to:** A decentralized, on-chain basis trade — like a hedge fund strategy packaged as a stablecoin.

**Fundamental Analyst Notes:**
- The delta-neutral strategy is clever but *not risk-free* — it depends on perpetual funding rates staying positive
- If funding rates go negative (bear market), USDe yield drops to near zero
- Ethena relies on CEXs (Binance, Bybit) for the short hedge — a centralization risk
- USDe is not a "stablecoin" in the traditional sense — it's a yield-bearing synthetic dollar
- The protocol has generated $500M+ in cumulative revenue — real yield, not emissions
