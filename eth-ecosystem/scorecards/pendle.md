## 🔍 TruthSeeker — 8/10

**What It Does:** Pendle tokenizes future yield into two tradeable components:
- **PT (Principal Token):** Receive the underlying asset at maturity (like a zero-coupon bond)
- **YT (Yield Token):** Receive all yield generated by the underlying asset until maturity

This lets users: lock in fixed yield (buy PT), speculate on yield going up (buy YT), or provide liquidity for fees.

**Comparable to:** There is no direct comparison — Pendle invented yield tokenization. It's the "options market for yield."

**Fundamental Analyst Notes:**
- Yield tokenization is a genuinely new DeFi primitive — no one else does this at scale
- Pendle's integration with Ethena (sUSDe) created a flywheel: fixed sUSDe yield became the "risk-free rate" of DeFi
- The vePENDLE model (vote-escrowed) aligns long-term holders with protocol governance
- As more yield-bearing assets emerge (LSTs, LRTs, RWA), Pendle's addressable market grows
