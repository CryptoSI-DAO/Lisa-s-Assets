## 🔍 TruthSeeker — 8/10

**What It Does:** Sky (rebranded from MakerDAO) is the protocol behind USDS (formerly DAI) — the largest decentralized stablecoin. The protocol lends USDS against ETH, stETH, and real-world assets (RWA) like US Treasury bills. Users can lock USDS in the DSR (Dai Savings Rate) to earn ~5-8% APY — yield backed by real economic activity, not token emissions.

**Comparable to:** The "central bank" of DeFi — Sky/USDS is the decentralized alternative to the Federal Reserve, with RWA yield as the "risk-free rate."

**Fundamental Analyst Notes:**
- RWA (real-world asset) integration is a genuine innovation — US Treasury bills back USDS
- DSR yield (~5-8%) is among the highest "risk-free" yields in DeFi
- The rebrand from Maker → Sky and DAI → USDS was controversial but positions for growth
- MKR → SKY token transition (1 MKR = 24,000 SKY) was confusing but increases accessibility
