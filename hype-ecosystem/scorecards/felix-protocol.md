## 🔍 TruthSeeker — 7/10

**What It Does:** Felix Protocol issues feUSD — an overcollateralized native stablecoin on HyperEVM. Users deposit collateral (HYPE, kHYPE, etc.) and borrow feUSD at a collateral ratio above 100%. The protocol also facilitates lending — depositors earn interest, borrowers pay it.

**Why It Matters:** Hyperliquid currently relies on *bridged* stablecoins (USDT0, USDe). A native stablecoin eliminates bridging risk, keeps yields on-chain, and provides a genuinely decentralized dollar for HL DeFi.

**Comparable to:** Lido's stETH + Aave's GHO — a native stablecoin lending protocol.
