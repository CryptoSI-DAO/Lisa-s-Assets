**What It Does:** OpenChat is a fully on-chain messaging and social platform running on ICP. Every message, group, and user profile is stored on-chain as canister data. The platform is governed by an SNS DAO — the community votes on upgrades, features, and treasury allocation. No central operator can censor, shut down, or moderate content.
